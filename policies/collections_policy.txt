LENDING POLICY: EARLY DELINQUENCY COLLECTIONS (1-30 DPD)
1. PTP Threshold: Maximum promise-to-pay date is strictly up to 7 days from the current date.
2. Part Payments: Accepted only if borrower commits >= 30% of outstanding EMI amount upfront, with remaining balance scheduled within 7 days.
3. Hardship & Restructuring: Any request for interest waiver, principal reduction, or tenure extension must be classified as 'hardship' and escalated immediately to the Credit Committee. Autonomous agents must never promise or approve waivers.
4. Disputes: Any claim of already paid, wrong debit, incorrect amount, or fraudulent ledger must be classified as 'dispute' and escalated immediately to the Dispute Resolution Desk with full call transcript.
5. Avoidance: Any customer hanging up or refusing to engage must be classified as 'avoidance' and scheduled for retry within 24 hours.
6. Tone: Firm, respectful, empathetic, strictly compliant with RBI Fair Practices Code.
